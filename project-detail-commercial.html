Project Overview

Elevate Your
Business Address

Rishi Business Square offers premium commercial spaces and retail units on College Road — Nashik's most sought-after corporate address. Designed for maximum visibility and footfall, it is the perfect launchpad for retail brands and growing enterprises.

With an elegant glass façade, grand entrance lobby, and flexible floor plans, it sets a new benchmark for corporate spaces in the city.

Why Choose Us

Designed for

Your workspace is a reflection of your brand's ambitions. We’ve designed Rishi Business Square with high-speed elevators, column-free office layouts, and a dedicated multi-level parking system.

The ground and first floors are optimized for high-visibility retail outlets, while the upper floors provide serene, productive environments for modern offices.
