Project Overview

Rishi Business Square brings modern retail and office spaces to College Road — one of Nashik's busiest commercial corridors. Designed across ground-plus-five floors, the project offers flexible retail units and business-ready office floors with high footfall visibility.

Every unit is designed around visibility, accessibility and flexible layouts, with premium finishes and a construction quality that's inspected at every stage — in keeping with our promise, Come Home To Quality.
