Project Overview

Your business. Your legacy.

A canvas where businesses plant roots and investments grow — on one of Nashik's fastest-emerging corridors.

Rishi Business Square brings modern retail and office spaces to College Road — one of Nashik's busiest commercial corridors. Designed across ground-plus-five floors, the project offers flexible retail units and business-ready office floors with high footfall visibility. Every unit is designed around visibility, accessibility and flexible layouts, with premium finishes and construction quality inspected at every stage.
