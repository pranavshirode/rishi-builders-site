Project Overview

Low-density, sunlit living

Premium 2 & 3 BHK homes designed for modern families in one of Nashik's most established residential neighbourhoods.

Rishi Sunrise Residency brings low-density, sunlit living to Panchavati — one of Nashik's most established residential neighbourhoods. Spread across a landscaped campus, the project offers thoughtfully planned 2 & 3 BHK homes with wide balconies, cross ventilation and a clubhouse built for everyday family life. Every home is designed around natural light and airflow, with premium fittings and a construction quality that's inspected at every stage.
