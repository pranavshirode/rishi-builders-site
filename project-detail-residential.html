Project Overview

Low-density,
sunlit living

Rishi Sunrise Residency brings low-density, sunlit living to Panchavati — one of Nashik's most established residential neighbourhoods. Spread across a landscaped campus, the project offers thoughtfully planned 2 & 3 BHK homes with wide balconies and cross ventilation.

Every home is designed around natural light and airflow, with premium fittings and a construction quality that's inspected at every stage.
