Project Overview

Rishi Sunrise Residency brings low-density, sunlit living to Panchavati — one of Nashik's most established residential neighbourhoods. Spread across a landscaped campus, the project offers thoughtfully planned 2 & 3 BHK homes with wide balconies, cross ventilation and a clubhouse built for everyday family life.

Every home is designed around natural light and airflow, with premium fittings and a construction quality that's inspected at every stage — in keeping with our promise, Come Home To Quality.
