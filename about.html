Company Introduction

Two Decades Of Building Trust, Not Just Structures

Rishi Builders & Developers was founded with a simple belief — that a home is more than four walls, it's a promise kept. Since our first project, we've grown into one of Nashik's trusted names in residential, commercial and land development.

Our team of architects, engineers and project managers work together on every site to make sure what's promised in the brochure is exactly what's delivered at possession — on quality, on specification, and on time.

Today, with 25+ projects delivered and thousands of families housed, our tagline remains our operating principle: Come Home To Quality.
