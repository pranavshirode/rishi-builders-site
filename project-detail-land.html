Project Overview

Your land. Your legacy.

A canvas where families plant roots and investments grow — on one of Nashik's fastest-emerging corridors.

Rishi Green Acres offers fully NA (Non-Agricultural), layout-approved plots on Trimbak Road — one of Nashik's fastest-emerging growth corridors. Each plot is title-clear and ready for construction, making it a straightforward option for building your own home or holding as a long-term investment. Surrounded by natural beauty and well-connected to Nashik city, this is where opportunity meets serenity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Rishi Green Acres — plots, approvals, and financing.

Are these plots NA (Non-Agricultural) approved?⌄
Yes, all plots at Rishi Green Acres are NA and layout approved, ready for residential construction.
Can I start construction immediately after booking?⌄
Yes, once registration is complete you can begin construction, subject to local municipal approvals.
Is bank loan financing available for these plots?⌄
Yes, our team can assist with plot-loan financing through partner banks and NBFCs.
What are the available plot sizes?⌄
Plot sizes range from 1500 sq.ft to 3000 sq.ft, with multiple orientations and corner plot options available.
Is there a site visit facility?⌄
Yes, we offer free guided site visits by appointment. You can book one using the enquiry form on this page or by calling our sales team directly.
