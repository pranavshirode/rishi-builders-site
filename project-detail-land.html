Project Overview

Build Your Dream
in Nature's Lap

Rishi Green Acres offers fully developed, clear-title NA plots situated on the scenic Trimbak Road. Surrounded by lush greenery and majestic hills, it provides a serene escape while remaining perfectly connected to Nashik city.

Whether you're looking to build a weekend villa or seeking a high-growth investment, these plots come with ready infrastructure, making your vision a reality instantly.

Investing in land is investing in certainty. Rishi Green Acres comes with 100% clear titles, separate 7/12 extracts for each plot, and Town Planning (TP) approval.
